# Inner Moon voice: the flirty Moon

The Moon is the brand's personality. She's a vintage glamour girl: she bats her lashes, pouts and teases. She is warm, witty and on your side. Copy personifies her and talks to the reader as "you".

## Five words
Flirty · warm · witty · encouraging · elegant

## How she sounds
- **Headlines personify the sky.** "The Moon is making eyes at your ambitions." "Venus would like a word." "Mercury left you on read."
- **Body copy speaks to "you"**, in short, confident sentences. One idea per slide.
- **Questions are gentle and specific.** They start with *What*, *Where* or *Who*, never *Why aren't you*.
- **Manifestation language stays mainstream and vision-forward:** intention, call in, align, ask, receive, open. It should be an easy read for someone new to astrology.
- **Wit lands with a soft finish.** Tease, then reassure: "Say the thing you've been rehearsing, kindly."

## She never
- Promises outcomes ("you will get the job", "money is coming").
- Predicts health, money, pregnancy, legal results or death.
- Shames, scares or doom-posts (no "brace yourself", "toxic", "punished").
- Is crude or sexual. Flirty means charming, not explicit.
- Uses astrology jargon without saying what it means.
- Uses emoji on slides. Captions may use at most 2, from: 🌙 ✨ 💫

## Words she likes / avoids
| Likes | Avoids |
| --- | --- |
| call in, invite, ask, glow, linger, flirt with, soften | manifest *guaranteed*, destiny *will*, cursed, chaos |
| kindly, gently, boldly, sweetly | literally, vibes (max once a week), insane |

## Slide formulas
- **Hook (slide 1):** kicker = day · Moon phase · season. Headline = the day's sky as a flirtation (≤ 60 chars). Sub = "Today's sky for the {Element} signs →".
- **Sign slides (2–4):** body = how today's sky touches the sign (≤ 140 chars). Question = one journal prompt (≤ 70 chars).
- **CTA (slide 5):** a *send* or *save* ask, not "follow us". For example: "Send this to your {Sign} friend." or "Save this for your next Full Moon."

## Captions
Open with the hook line rephrased, then 1–2 lines of context and a save/send ask, ending with 3–5 specific hashtags (never #reels, #explore or #astrology alone). If a post shows a realistic AI person, label it.
